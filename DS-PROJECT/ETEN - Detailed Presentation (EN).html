What ETEN is

Who sustains the translation.

ETEN funds most of Shemá’s resources. Each year we account for the progress: how many chapters each project translated. This report is the basis for the next funding cycle.

Automatic counting

The credits count themselves.

For each project on the list: chapters translated by year-end, minus those at the end of the prior year. The difference is the credits earned.

No manual spreadsheet: just keep the progress updated in the projects.
