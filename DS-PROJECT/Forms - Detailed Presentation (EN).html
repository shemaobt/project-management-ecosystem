Health Assessment · The four dimensions

What is asked in each one.

01 · Emotional
How are you feeling in your hearts?
"How have you been sleeping? Is anything taking your sleep or peace? Where do you find rest?"
02 · Relational
How are the relationships among you?
"Any unresolved conflicts? How do you care for each other? Anyone isolated? And with the community?"
03 · Spiritual
How is the walk with God?
"How is the prayer life? Can you worship together? What has God been saying?"
04 · Physical
How is physical health?
"Anyone with health problems? Food, water? Have you rested? Any risks?"

Each dimension gets a status — Good, Attention or Critical — and at the end the app suggests pastoral care.
