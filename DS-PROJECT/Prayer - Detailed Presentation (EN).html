Where the requests come from

Prayer is born in the field.

The requests arrive automatically from the two forms the teams fill in — no manual typing. Each submission feeds the wall.

Monthly · by the field leader
Monthly Pulse

The "Prayer request" card — text or audio. The team chooses whether to authorize sharing with the network.

Quarterly · by the Facilitator
Health Assessment

During the meeting, the team’s prayer request is also recorded and goes to the wall.
