What the Rhythm tab is

Shemá means "listen".

Each meeting is a step in that listening — information flows from the field to leadership, and care flows back. Forms feed the meetings; meetings feed the decisions.

↑
Information rises

Progress, health and needs of the field teams.

↓
Care descends

Decisions, resources, prayer and direction return to the teams.

◷
On the calendar

Cycles aligned to Q1–Q4. Each meeting has its cadence.

How to track

Every meeting, tracked.

By region, the tab shows the state of each meeting and whether the teams have reported — so you know, at a glance, what is ready and what is overdue.

On track

Held in the current period.

To do

Still within the period’s deadline.

Overdue

The period passed without a record.

Readiness

"9/12 reported" — before meeting.

Log a meeting

One click, into the history.

When you hold a meeting, log the date and a note. The status becomes "On track" and the next occurrence is calculated automatically.

All saved in the app — the history is kept by region.
