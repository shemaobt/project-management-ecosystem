Full Update

Five short questions the leader answers on the phone: how the team is, how many chapters advanced, what is missing, a prayer request and a photo.

Works offline and returns by WhatsApp or file. On arrival, it automatically updates the Progress, Health and Needs tabs.

Filled in by the team leader, every month.
